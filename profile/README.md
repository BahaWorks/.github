## Welcome to BahaWorks 👋

BahaWorks builds websites for real people: multilingual, fast and accessible on any device.

### Our project

**[Maple Steps](https://maplesteps.ca)** is a free guide for newcomers to Canada, available in English, French, Arabic, Chinese, Hindi and Persian. It explains first steps like getting a SIN, opening a bank account and finding settlement services, in plain language.

### What we focus on

- **Multilingual:** content that works in several languages, including right-to-left languages like Arabic and Persian
- **Accessible:** readable for everyone, including people using screen readers or slow connections
- **Fast and simple:** lightweight pages that load quickly on phones
